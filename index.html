Meet the Author

Sridhar H. Patel

Sridhar H. Patel is an author, professional, husband, and father dedicated to translating timeless wisdom and practical science into simple daily habits for modern life.

Growing up surrounded by the quiet discipline of his parents, Bina and Hemendra Patel, and his two grandmothers—both of whom lived vibrant, active lives well past their nineties—Sridhar saw firsthand how daily mindfulness, purposeful work, and clean living build a life of lasting strength. After experiencing the fast rush and pressure of a demanding modern career himself, he returned to those foundational roots to reclaim his own clarity, energy, and peace of mind.

Through his books—UNFED, STILLNESS IN MOTION, THE DAILY MINDFUL PAUSE, and HANUMAN—he bridges the gap between ancient philosophy and everyday vitality so readers of all ages can cultivate a calm mind, a strong body, and a purposeful legacy.

Sridhar lives with his wife, Deval Patel, PhD, and their two sons, Taarush and Ishan, who remain his greatest inspiration.

About GrowWisePress

"Igniting Curiosity. Preserving Culture. Simple Truths for Real Growth."

GrowWisePress is an independent publishing house dedicated to creating meaningful books for modern minds. Our mission is simple: to bridge the gap between timeless wisdom and today's generation. Whether it is sharing simple habits for lifelong health, translating ancient philosophy for the modern desk, or retelling cultural stories for children, every book we publish is designed to spark curiosity, strengthen the body, and inspire positive action across generations.

We believe the best books help families grow wiser, stronger, and closer to their roots.
